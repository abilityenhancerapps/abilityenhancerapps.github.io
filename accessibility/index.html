Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and our apps.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. These are worldwide rules for making websites easier to use for people with disabilities.

What we do

  • Large, clear text and strong colour contrast.
  • Works with a keyboard only, and with screen readers.
  • Works on small screens and when you zoom in.
  • Follows your device’s dark mode and reduced motion settings.
  • Pictures have text descriptions.
  • Plain, simple language.
  • No pop-ups, no forms and no moving content.

Report a problem

If something is hard to use, please tell us. Email abilityenhancerapps@gmail.com. Tell us which page, what happened, and what device or assistive technology you use, if you can.

This statement was last reviewed on October 23, 2026.

Ability Enhancer Apps is a free, independent project. Our apps are not medical devices and do not give medical advice.
