Meet Dhaan Saathi, a free, offline rice photo checker.

This experimental app needs field testing.

Take a clear photo of the rice plant.

The checker runs on your phone, without an internet connection.

The result is a model suggestion, not a confirmed diagnosis.

It may confuse diseases, damage, or nutrient problems.

Results stay silent by default.

Tap Hear results if you want speech.

Use Stop speaking to end playback.

Home returns to the starting screen for another photo.

Close exits the app. These are separate controls.

Use English or Hindi.

Read the result on screen, or choose to hear it when you need spoken help.

The checker can be confidently wrong.

A healthy result is not proof that a plant is disease-free.

Ask a local agricultural expert to assess suspected problems.

We welcome agricultural organisations and rice growers

who can help evaluate this experimental tool under real field conditions.

Dhaan Saathi. A project by Dr. Chindanoor Rajesh.
