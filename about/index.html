About Ability Enhancer Apps

Ability Enhancer Apps is a free, independent project. We build simple apps that help people with disabilities.

How we work

  • Our apps are free, with no ads.
  • They keep your information private, on your own device.
  • They work without the internet.
  • We listen to the people who use them.

No medical advice

Our apps and this website do not give medical advice. They are not medical devices. Always talk to your doctor, speech therapist or other health professional about your health.
