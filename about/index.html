About Ability Enhancer Apps

Ability Enhancer Apps is the passion project of Dr. Chindanoor Rajesh.

The aim is simple: free apps for people with all kinds of disabilities.

Our apps

  • Speak My Talk learns how you say your words, then shows them big and says them clearly.
  • RaahiSense warns blind and low-vision people about obstacles ahead, out loud.

Contact

Email us at abilityenhancerapps@gmail.com.

Ability Enhancer Apps is a free, independent project. Our apps are not medical devices and do not give medical advice.

Questions, ideas or want to help test? We'd love to hear from you. Email us at abilityenhancerapps@gmail.com.
