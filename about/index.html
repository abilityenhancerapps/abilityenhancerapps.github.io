About Ability Enhancer Apps

Ability Enhancer Apps is the passion project of Dr. Chindanoor Rajesh.

The aim is practical work for real problems. Free apps for people with disabilities are an important part of that. The project also includes a rice-farming experiment, and projects that are not apps.

Projects

  • Speak My Talk is for people whose speech is hard for others to understand. It learns how you say your words, then shows them big and says them clearly.
  • RaahiSense is for blind and low-vision people. It warns about obstacles ahead, out loud, and is meant to be used with a white cane.
  • Dhaan Saathi (धान साथी) is a free, experimental offline Android app. It looks at a photo of a rice plant and suggests possible diseases or nutrient issues. It is not field validated, and it does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
  • Projects Beyond Apps is for work that is not a phone app. The first project there is a low-cost, solar-powered machine that pulls drinking water from the air. The prototype is still being built.
