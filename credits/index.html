Photograph in the video

A confirmation screen in the Dhaan Saathi video shows leaf_blast(21).jpg, source-labelled leaf blast, 480×640, from Rice Leaf Disease Classification Bd / IoT-RiceMobileNet. It is shown as a photo to confirm. The video does not present that label as a diagnosis, and it does not show a model score.

Creators cited by the dataset card: Khawja Imran Masud, Md Yasin Zihad, Mehedi Hasan Shuvo, Mst Raonik Jannat, Jia Uddin, and Sahara Ali. Masud et al. Opening pictures and statistics

The first 26.8 seconds of the video use generated illustrations. They are not photographs of real farmers, and they are not field evidence. The figures in that opening come from published sources, listed in the statistic sources.

App screens

Phone screens later in the video, and the stills on the Dhaan Saathi page, are genuine v6 Android emulator captures. They are not photographs of a physical phone.
