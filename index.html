Free projects for accessibility, farming and other practical work

Ability Enhancer Apps is the passion project of Dr. Chindanoor Rajesh. It makes free, private apps for people with disabilities, and it also includes farming and other practical, real-world projects.

Support for people with disabilities stays important here. Each of those apps is still for the people named on its own page. To our knowledge, no other apps, paid or free, currently offer similar capabilities to Speak My Talk and RaahiSense.

What we believe

  • Free. Our apps cost nothing. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
  • Private. In our apps, your information stays on your own phone or tablet. We do not collect it.
  • Works offline. Our apps work without the internet.
  • Independent. This is a small, independent passion project. No company or investor owns it.

Our projects

  • Speak My Talk

    For people whose speech is hard for others to understand. The app learns how you say your words, then shows them in big letters and says them out loud in a clear voice.

    Status: early version (prototype).

    Learn more about Speak My Talk

  • RaahiSense

    RaahiSense is a free phone app for blind and low-vision people. It uses the phone’s camera to spot obstacles ahead, such as people, vehicles, poles and walls, and warns out loud in several languages. It works offline and is meant to be used alongside a white cane, not instead of one.

    Status: early version (prototype). Android first.

    Learn more about RaahiSense

  • Dhaan Saathi (धान साथी)

    A free, experimental Android app for rice. You take a photo of a rice plant, and the app suggests possible diseases or nutrient issues. It works offline, in English or Hindi. It can be wrong.

    Status: experimental. Not field validated. A closed test is planned. Not on Google Play yet.

    Learn more about Dhaan Saathi

  • Projects Beyond Apps

    Not every problem needs an app. Our first project beyond apps is a low-cost, solar-powered machine that pulls drinking water from the air.

    Status: We are in the process of building the prototype.

    Learn more about our projects beyond apps
